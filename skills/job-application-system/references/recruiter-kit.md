# Recruiter reply kit

The kit is what goes into every recruiter reply. Fill it once from
setup, truthfully, and reuse it. Nothing in the kit is invented.

## Kit fields

- Full name: [YOUR NAME]
- Phone: [YOUR PHONE]
- Availability window: [DAYS, TIMES, TIMEZONE]
- Work authorization wording: [EXACT WORDING, e.g. authorized to
  work in the US without sponsorship]
- Location for forms: [CITY, STATE]
- Current employer and title: [EMPLOYER, TITLE] (never removed
  from application history)
- Track LinkedIn profile: [URL for this track only]
- Resume: the tailored resume for the target role (see below)
- Salary rule for this track: [STATE TARGET PROACTIVELY / NEVER
  NAME A NUMBER, from setup]

## Autopilot rules

- Any recruiter message, in any inbox, gets read, and a JD-tailored
  reply with a tailored resume and the full kit is SENT without
  per-reply approval.
- The reply is tailored to the actual job description: labels from
  the posting, per-skill years stated separately, honest fit notes
  where the fit is partial.
- Report the sent reply one line: recruiter, company, role, and
  confirmation that To and attachment were verified.

## Hold rules

HOLD, never autopilot, and surface to the human:

- RTR (right-to-represent) signatures.
- ID documents of any kind (copies, photos, scans).
- Any invented fact: an employer, date, metric, certification, or
  KPI the human has not confirmed.
- Any salary or rate the human has not discussed.
- Any question with no truthful answer.

A held item waits for the human's explicit decision. It is never
sent quietly and never dropped quietly.

## Send verification (every send, no exceptions)

1. Before sending, check Sent and Drafts for the thread. If a send
   is already verified in Sent, trash the draft and send nothing.
2. Reply ONLY to the other party's inbound message, never to the
   human's own sent copy. Corrections to sent mail are fresh sends.
3. After sending, read back the saved email and verify the To
   header and the attachment. A send is not confirmed until the
   read-back matches.
4. A send timeout says NOTHING about delivery. Never retry on a
   timeout or a killed batch until Sent mail has been checked for
   that recipient. Allow a few minutes for the send to land before
   concluding it is absent.
5. Email bodies containing amounts go through a safe path that
   preserves the amounts exactly. Verify amounts on read-back.
6. No em dashes in anything sent out.

## Duplicate-role rule

When two recruiters pitch the same role, reply to each
independently. Never tell one recruiter that another pitched the
same role. Never flag a double submission.

## Postponement rule

When postponing or rescheduling anything, the reason is ALWAYS "a
family emergency". Never "traveling", never "a family event".

## Warm threads

- A recruiter thread with an open question, a pending reply, or a
  promised follow-up is a warm thread. Warm threads are checked on
  every sweep and every weekly pass.
- The human's "go ahead" on a held item means send it as-is, with
  clarifying questions only. Do not re-draft a go-ahead item into a
  new version without being asked.
- If a recruiter re-asks for something already refused (for
  example, ID documents after a written refusal), surface it to the
  human. Do not re-send the refusal on autopilot and do not comply.
