# Platform playbooks

General rules first (from SKILL.md): 24-hour freshness, fill ALL
questions, one verification attempt max, blocked attempts never
consume a track number, never sign out of a session once signed in.

## LinkedIn

- Quick-apply roles: complete every question, including optional
  ones, before submitting. A quick apply that skips questions is
  not a submission under this system.
- Company-site roles found via LinkedIn: apply on the company site,
  not through the LinkedIn shortcut, when the posting offers both.
- Track identity: use the track's LinkedIn profile only. Never send
  another track's profile URL.
- Recruiter messages on LinkedIn go through the recruiter autopilot
  (see `references/recruiter-kit.md`) with the photo rule applied.
- Connection requests to staffing recruiters run in pilot batches
  (see SKILL.md section 10). Measure accepts and replies before
  scaling.

## Indeed

- Start every blitz session with the Indeed digest: the fresh
  postings for the track, filtered to the last 24 hours.
- Indeed-hosted applications: complete the full application, not
  just the resume send.
- Employer-site redirects: follow through to the employer site and
  complete the application there; a redirect click is not a
  submission.
- Skip-listed employers are skipped at the digest stage, before any
  application work starts.

## Monster

- Session preservation is critical: once signed in, never sign out
  at the end of a task. Leave the session alive for the next wave.
- If a saved password is rejected, stop and surface it to the
  human. Do not cycle password guesses and do not create a second
  account.
- If the session is lost, re-authentication is a human-hands task
  (the human may need to complete a phone or SMS step). Do not
  route around identity checks.

## Dice

- Dice applications need the human's sign-in or reset when access
  lapses. Surface it once, clearly, and move to other routes while
  waiting. Do not stall the whole wave on one platform.
- Apply the same freshness and full-question rules as every route.

## Company career sites

- Preferred route when a posting offers both a board apply and a
  direct site apply.
- Expect account creation, screening questions, and sometimes
  assessments. Complete the full flow; a half-finished portal
  profile is not a submission.
- Record the confirmation (number, email, or screenshot reference)
  in the registry entry.
- Some portals show a "next steps" email after submission (screening
  chats, assessments). Surface those to the human the same day;
  they are human-hands items, not autopilot items.

## We Work Remotely

- Remote-only board. Geography gate still applies: remote within the
  approved region only.
- Many postings link out to company sites. Follow through and
  complete the company-site application.

## Wellfound

- Startup-focused board. Profiles matter here: keep the track's
  Wellfound profile current before a wave.
- Same freshness, full-question, and registry rules as every route.

## Hiring Cafe

- Aggregator-style board. Verify the role is still open on the
  employer's own site before applying, since aggregator listings
  go stale faster than direct postings.
- Skip-list and freshness rules apply at the aggregator stage.

## Route selection per session

A session uses the routes that are healthy that day. A route that is
blocked (sign-in lost, CAPTCHA loop, page removed) is skipped for
the session, recorded in the registry notes, and retried next
session. A blocked route never consumes track numbers and never
blocks the other routes.
