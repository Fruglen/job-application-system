# LinkedIn DM template

Voice: natural, warm opener, one genuine observation, one easy ask,
signed with the first name. Never paste your own LinkedIn URL in a
LinkedIn DM. No em dashes.

## First connection note (300 characters max)

Hi [FIRST NAME], I came across your work on [SPECIFIC, TRUE DETAIL:
a post, a role you staff, a team you hire for]. I am a [TRACK TITLE]
focused on [ONE AREA]. Would love to connect.

## First DM after acceptance

Hi [FIRST NAME], thanks for connecting.

I noticed [ONE GENUINE OBSERVATION: a role you are hiring for, a
post you wrote, a team or client you staff for]. I work in [TRACK
AREA], with strengths in [ONE OR TWO SKILLS, truthful values only].

Would you be open to a short chat about [THE SPECIFIC ROLE OR AREA]?
I am available [DAYS, TIMES, TIMEZONE] and happy to work around your
schedule.

Thanks,
[YOUR FIRST NAME]

## Follow-up DM (only if no reply after about a week)

Hi [FIRST NAME], just floating this back up in case it got buried.
I am still interested in [THE SPECIFIC ROLE OR AREA] if the timing
works on your side. No pressure either way.

Thanks,
[YOUR FIRST NAME]

## Rules

- One genuine observation means something actually seen on their
  profile or in their message. Never invent a detail to sound
  personal.
- One easy ask per message. Never stack asks.
- Watch replies MUST include the profile photo. If the photo cannot
  attach, HOLD the reply unless the human waived the photo for that
  specific thread.
- Proactive outreach runs in pilot-sized batches. Measure accepts
  and replies before scaling.
- If a thread goes cold after two follow-ups, stop. Log it and move
  on.
