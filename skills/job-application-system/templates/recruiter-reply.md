line that does not apply. Never invent a fact to fill a bracket.

- [SKILL LABEL]: [YEARS] years

one sentence. Labels from the posting, ordered by employer priority.)

Location: [CITY, STATE]

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]

Checklist before sending (see `references/recruiter-kit.md`):

- [ ] Replying to the recruiter's inbound message, not to own mail
- [ ] Sent and Drafts checked for this thread, no duplicate send
- [ ] Resume attached is the tailored version for this role
- [ ] To header and attachment verified on read-back after sending
- [ ] No em dashes anywhere in the body
- [ ] No salary or rate included unless the track rule allows it
- [ ] No portfolio link in the body (resumes and forms only)
